# Voice-over: recording sheet

For: Santram, at the mic. The words are locked (cut v9, [video-script.md](video-script.md)); this
sheet is how to say them. Record each part as one continuous story first (next section), then
retake single lines only where needed; the editor picks the take that fits.

## Before you start

- **Room:** small, soft room (clothes, curtains, a bed), fans and phone notifications off. Early
  morning or late evening is quietest in the village.
- **Phone:** voice memo app, phone about a hand's width (15–20 cm) from your mouth, a little to the
  side so breath doesn't hit it. Same place and distance for every line.
- **Each take:** say the line's id first ("a-one, take two"), wait one second, say the line, wait one
  second. Bad take: don't stop the recording, pause, and say the whole line again.
- **Takes:** for each line, one take as you'd naturally say it, one a little slower with more
  weight on the bold words, and one with a smile. The smile take usually wins.
- **Overall feel:** proud and warm, not sad. You're showing what you built for your village, not
  asking for sympathy. Smile while you speak, even on the serious lines; it's heard.
- **Pace:** about 130 words a minute. If a take runs past the "longest" time, it can still be sped
  up slightly (up to 1.1×, voice unchanged), so choose the take that *sounds* best.

## Continuous takes: read each part as one story

Lines recorded one by one, in different sittings, sound joined in the video: the mood jumps from
one line to the next. So record each part **straight through, as one paragraph**, the way you'd
tell it to someone, and let the tone change by itself as the story moves. Do the whole read 2–3
times in the same sitting; the editor cuts the lines apart at your pauses and lays each on its shot.

How to read the paragraphs below:

- `/` a short breath (a comma's worth); `//` a full pause, about one second (the editor cuts here
  and the picture changes). Never run two lines together across a `//`.
- The small grey ids (`a1`, `t1`…) are only there so we can talk about a line; don't say them.
- **Bold** words carry the weight. Smile through all of it; slow down where it says so.
- Some words are **spelled the way to say them**, because they didn't come through clearly last
  time: *laaack* (lack), *ee-quips* (equips), *vil-lij* (village), *Maarg* (Marg), *Myooz Sparrk*
  (Muse Spark: say the r), *Lah-ma Garrd* (Llama Guard: say the r), *Bruj* (Braj), *HEK-sa-gonal*
  (hexagonal), *un-checkt* (unchecked: say the t), *an open-weightss* (say the a and the s), **in**
  model calls, *Maarg's · reach* (two words, not one), and *TYOO-ter* (tutor). Read them exactly as
  written.
- If one line goes wrong, don't restart the part: pause, say that line again *starting from the
  line before it*, and carry on.

### Words to watch (from the 2026-10-31 session)

A speech recognizer misheard these in every take, so a judge might too:

| Say | It came out as | How to fix it |
|---|---|---|
| don't **lack** talent | "don't like talent" (reverses the meaning) | open vowel as in "back": *laaack* |
| it **equips** | "it keeps" / "it takes" | don't swallow the start: *ee-KWIPS* |
| The world is **the** curriculum | "The world is curriculum" | say both "the"s |
| my **village** | "my values" / "my beliefs" | two clear syllables, slower: *VIL-lij* |
| **Llama Guard** · **Muse Spark** | "Lalmagard", "lalma guide" · "music spark", "news is part" | a tiny gap, and say the r: *Lah-ma… Garrd*, *Myooz… Sparrk* |
| nothing goes out **unchecked** | "uncheck" | finish the word: *un-checkt* |
| **in** model calls | "and model calls" | a clear short *in* |
| Marg's **reach** | "Margreach" | two words: *Maarg's · reach* |
| not a **tutor** | "not a total" | two clear parts: *TYOO-ter* |
| **Marg** | "mark" / "marl" | a long "aa", soft "g": *Maarg* (मार्ग) |
| **hexagonal** | (clear last time; keep it that way) | *HEK-sa-gonal* |
| **Braj** | "press" / "brush" | land the "j": *Bruj* |
| **an** open-**weights** model · no **app** | "and open weight model" · "apps" | *an* (not "and"), and finish with the s: *weightss*; one *app* |

**Pace:** last time it was 170–200 words a minute, and up to 240 when smiling. Keep the smile, and
slow down most on `a8`, the second half of `h9`, and `i5`.

## After recording

Send all the takes as they are (no editing, no music). The editor chooses one take per line, fits
it to the shots, and builds the next cut. A slight speed-up is used only where a take runs past its
"longest" time.
