# Voice-over: recording sheet

For: Santram, at the mic. The words are locked (cut v9, [video-script.md](video-script.md)); this
sheet is how to say them. Record each line on its own, in 2–3 takes, and the editor picks the take
that fits.

## Before you start

- **Room:** small, soft room (clothes, curtains, a bed), fans and phone notifications off. Early
  morning or late evening is quietest in the village.
- **Phone:** voice memo app, phone about a hand's width (15–20 cm) from your mouth, a little to the
  side so breath doesn't hit it. Same place and distance for every line.
- **Each take:** say the line's id first ("a-one, take two"), wait one second, say the line, wait one
  second. Bad take: don't stop the recording, pause, and say the whole line again.
- **Takes:** for each line, one take as you'd naturally say it, one a little slower with more
  weight on the bold words, and one with a smile. The smile take usually wins.
- **Overall feel:** proud and warm, not sad. You're showing what you built for your village, not
  asking for sympathy. Smile while you speak, even on the serious lines; it's heard.
- **Pace:** about 130 words a minute. If a take runs past the "longest" time, it can still be sped
  up slightly (up to 1.1×, voice unchanged), so choose the take that *sounds* best.

## After recording

Send all the takes as they are (no editing, no music). The editor chooses one take per line, fits
it to the shots, and builds the next cut. A slight speed-up is used only where a take runs past its
"longest" time.
