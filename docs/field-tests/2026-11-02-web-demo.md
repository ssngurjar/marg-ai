# Field test: a teacher, parents and students, 2 November 2026

For: judges and reviewers who want to know what happened when real people used Marg without a
script, and what changed because of it. The first field test, on WhatsApp the evening before, is
[2026-11-01-village-whatsapp.md](2026-11-01-village-whatsapp.md).

No message is quoted word for word, and no name, nickname or place from the sessions appears here
(CLAUDE.md §1). What people wrote is described in our own words; the cases added to tests and
banks are synthetic ones that check the same thing.

## Setup

- **Who:** in the morning, a government-school teacher from a district in Rajasthan, who spoke
  Hindi by voice note, and a friend of the owner who used Marg as a parent, in English. In the
  late afternoon, a group of mostly students, and one person on WhatsApp who started as a
  student, deleted their data and came back as a parent. All unscripted: nobody was told what
  to type.
- **How:** the web demo (`/demo`) and WhatsApp, the same pipeline, served from the owner's laptop
  through a Cloudflare tunnel. The same code and the same models as the deployed app.
- **When:** 2 November, about 11:41 to 13:22 and 16:37 to 17:18 IST: seven people, in ten
  accounts (nine browsers, one WhatsApp number; a browser reset starts a new account). With the
  first field test's one person, 8 real people took part.

## What people did that the banks never had

- The teacher gave the classes they teach as a **range in Hindi words**, by voice, twice, before
  trying it in digits.
- Sent **voice notes that came through empty** while learning the record button.
- Answered a chapter answer with a plain **ok**.
- The parent gave a first child **older than eight**, then added **another child with the name
  and age in the same message**.
- Asked, twice, **how Marg can help their child**, right after saying no to child talk.
- Tapped **Done** on a khoj before doing it, then said it hadn't been done yet.
- Asked Marg to **explain a school science topic** for an older child.
- Said they weren't comfortable with their child using AI (Marg answered from its reviewed facts:
  the child talks to Marg only after the parent says yes).
- Said **goodbye in chat shorthand** in the middle of the day.
- Wrote a short review of the web demo's **voice recording**: it wasn't clear when it was
  recording or how to send.
- In the afternoon: typed their **role before agreeing** to the privacy note; answered the note
  with a **misspelt yes and a question** about who made Marg; asked who made Marg as their
  **very first message**.
- Greeted a student mentor with a plain **"hi"**, more than once.
- A student said they wanted to **use Marg as a parent** that day.
- After a khoj, said **thanks** and then **"I'll tell you later"**.
- Asked about **exams not in Marg's sources** (a staff-selection exam), streams after class 10,
  engineering and caring for people, and made a plan.

Twenty problems, all fixed and tested on 2 November. One more waits on the owner: official
sources for the staff-selection exam, which Marg rightly said it had no verified details on.
Each fix has a test with a synthetic case, and the ones about reading people are in the banks
(after the last fixes: parent 249 of 252, student 58 of 59, teacher 45 of 45, onboarding 105 of
105).

## What went well

- No turn failed. Once the teacher chose Hindi, every reply came in Hindi.
- The teacher's chapter answers, in Hindi from a voice note, linked the chapter to real work and
  gave a five-minute class activity.
- A parent's discomfort with AI got an honest answer from Marg's reviewed facts, not a sales line.
- A student who asked about an exam Marg has no source for was told so plainly, and still got
  a free first step.
- One person deleted everything and started again in another role, and the khoj flow worked
  end to end for their child.

## What this does not show

A few people for a day is not evidence that Marg changes anything for a child; it shows what
breaks when real people use it, and that it can be fixed quickly. The first field test said the
same: that needs families using Marg for months.
