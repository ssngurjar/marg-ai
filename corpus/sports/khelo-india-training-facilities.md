## What it is

Khelo India Training Facilities is one part of the Khelo India scheme. The page describes its goal as "Building a future-ready sports ecosystem through world-class training infrastructure, athlete pathways, and grassroots development across India."

## Key numbers

- ₹10,586 Cr: the 5-year outlook for modernising sports infrastructure.
- 1066 Khelo India Centres are operational, with more being added.
- 35 Khelo India Centres of Excellence, one nodal point in every state and union territory.
- 23 Youth Sports Companies are planned under the 5-year expansion.

## Three levels of training

The page arranges training places in three levels, from the village up to the top.

**Grassroots level:**
- Utkrishta Vidyalayas
- Khelo India Feeder Schools
- Khelo India Centres

**Intermediate level:**
- National Centres of Excellence (NCOEs)
- SAI Training Centres
- Accredited Academies and Khelo India Accredited Academies
- Sports Companies
- Khelo India Centres of Excellence

**Elite level:**
- Olympic Training Centres

## Finding talent with technology

The page says grassroots talent identification will use AI-enabled assessment. This will be linked to the National Sports Repository System (NSRS) and the APAAR ID, so that an athlete's progress can be tracked over many years.

## What the page does not say

This page does not say who is eligible for each centre or how to apply. For those details, check the Khelo India website, www.kheloindia.gov.in.
