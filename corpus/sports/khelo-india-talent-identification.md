## What it is

Talent Identification and Development is one part of the Khelo India scheme. Its aim, in the page's words, is "Building a technology-enabled talent pathway that identifies, nurtures, and supports athletes from grassroots participation to elite performance."

## The four stages of an athlete's path

The page describes four stages that an athlete moves through:

1. Foundation
2. Grassroots
3. Intermediate
4. Elite

## How many athletes are supported

The page gives the current numbers of athletes at each level:

- Grassroots: 30,000
- Intermediate: 10,000
- Elite (TOPS/TAGG): 250

The plan for the coming years has a 5-year outlay of ₹2,407 Cr. It plans for 9,000 Khelo India Athletes (KIA) and 18,000 Emerging Khelo India Athletes (E-KIA).

## How athletes are assessed and supported

- Assessment Camps: "Standardized evaluation and athlete monitoring".
- OPA Incentives: "Performance linked Out-of-Pocket Allowance support". This means athletes who perform well get an allowance for their own expenses.

## Who finds the talent

- The Talent Identification and Development Committee (TIDC) looks after national policy and oversight.
- Talent Identification Zonal Committees (TIZC) coordinate the work in each region and evaluate talent.
- Talent Scouts find and nurture grassroots talent through scouting and tracking performance.

## What the page does not say

This page does not give age limits, eligibility rules or the steps for applying. Those details should be checked on the official Khelo India website, www.kheloindia.gov.in.
