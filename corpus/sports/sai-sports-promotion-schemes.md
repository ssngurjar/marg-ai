## What it is

The Sports Authority of India (SAI) runs several sports promotion schemes for the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports. This page lists four of them under "Sports Promotion Schemes".

## SAI Training Centres (STC)

SAI Training Centres "groom the junior level sports persons in the age group of 10-18 years." They are meant for young athletes who are still at the junior level.

## Extension Centres of STC

These centres are "established to develop sports standard in schools and colleges having requisite basic sports infrastructure." This means a school or college that already has basic sports facilities can host an extension centre.

## National Sports Talent Contest (NSTC)

The NSTC scheme is "being implemented to scout sports talent in the age group of 8-14 years from schools." It looks for sporting talent among school children of this age.

## National Centre of Excellence (NCOE)

NCOEs exist "to train athletes to achieve excellence in Olympics and other International Events."

## What the page does not say

The page does not explain how athletes are selected for these schemes, how to apply, or any money amounts. SAI publishes selection trial notices separately on its website, sportsauthorityofindia.nic.in.
