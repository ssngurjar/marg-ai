## What it is

Khelo India is India's flagship national scheme for sports development at the grassroots. The scheme works to bring more people into sport, to find and develop talented athletes, and to build sports facilities across the country.

## How Khelo India grew

- 2017: the Khelo India Programme was approved and launched.
- 2018: the first School Games and the Athlete Scholarship Scheme started.
- 2019: the Youth Games expanded and talent identification was scaled up.
- 2020: the University Games and Winter Games were launched.
- 2021: Khelo India Centres expanded across the country.
- 2022: State Centres of Excellence were launched.
- 2023: Para Games, the ASMITA League and indigenous sports were added.
- 2024: Beach Games were launched.
- 2025: Excellence Schools and athlete pathways were strengthened.
- 2026: phase 3.0 of the Khelo India Scheme was launched under Khelo Bharat Niti.

## Aims for 2026 to 2031

The page lists six aims: better performance in international sport, stronger sports infrastructure and talent pathways, using sport for social development (inclusion, health, education, community), making sport "A People's Movement" with wide public participation, linking Khelo Bharat Niti 2025 with the National Education Policy 2020, and using science and technology in coaching and governance. The long-term targets are a top 10 rank in the Olympics by 2036 and a top 5 rank by 2048.

## The nine parts of the scheme

1. Strengthening Strategic Sports Assets
2. Sports Infrastructure for Medal Strategy
3. State Action Plan for Sports Infrastructure Development
4. Sports Competitions and Leagues
5. Khelo India Training Facilities
6. Talent Identification and Development
7. Coach and Support Staff Development
8. Sports Technology
9. Fit India Movement
