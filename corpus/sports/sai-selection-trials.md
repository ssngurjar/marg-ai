## What it is

The Sports Authority of India (SAI) has a "Selection Trial" page on its website. SAI posts notices there when it holds trials to pick athletes for its training centres. Each notice has a title, a publication date and a link to a file with the full details.

## What kind of trials are posted

The notices on the page (as seen on 28 October 2026) show the kinds of trials SAI holds:

- Selection trials for hockey, residential, for boys and girls, at Ranchi in 2026.
- Selection trials for archery, residential, for boys and girls, at SAI Training Centre (STC) Ranchi. This trial was held on 26 and 27 October 2026, so it is already over.
- Induction trials 2026-27 for residential and non-residential athletes in hockey and archery at a SAI Training Centre.
- Selection trials for admission to SAI National Centre of Excellence (NCOE) Rohtak, for Under-15 and Under-17 boxing categories, session 2026-27.

"Residential" means the athlete lives at the centre. "Non-residential" means the athlete trains there but lives at home.

## How to use the page

The page itself does not explain who can apply, the age rules or the steps to apply. These are written inside each notice file. A student who wants to try for a SAI centre should open the latest notice for their sport on sportsauthorityofindia.nic.in and read its eligibility, dates and venue carefully. Trial dates pass quickly, so check the date on the notice before planning to go.
