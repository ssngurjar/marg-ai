## What it is

National Centres of Excellence (NCOEs) are run by the Sports Authority of India (SAI). At these centres SAI trains "promising athletes" for the Olympics and other international events. The page says NCOEs "operate as regular coaching camps for the best available talent in India."

## What athletes get

According to the page, athletes at an NCOE get:

- "state of the art infrastructure and playing facilities"
- "sports science backup"
- an "individualized diet"
- coaching from the best coaches, with qualified support staff and High Performance Directors

The page also mentions a Rs. 80.00 Crores scientific set-up that covers areas such as anthropometry (body measurement), biomechanics (how the body moves) and physiotherapy.

## Who it is for

The page says an NCOE "is capable of accommodating elite to development athletes". This means both top athletes and developing athletes can train there. The page says NCOEs "cover 23 focused/priority disciplines" (sports).

NCOEs also keep layers of upcoming sportspersons in training, "giving a wider choice of talent and continuity for selection to National Teams."

## How to get in

This page does not give an age limit or explain how athletes are selected. SAI announces selection trials for its centres on a separate "Selection Trial" page of its website, sportsauthorityofindia.nic.in.
