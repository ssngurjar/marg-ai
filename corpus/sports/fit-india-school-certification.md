## What it is

Fit India School certification is part of the Fit India Movement, which was launched on 29th August 2019. It wants schools to be the first place after home where physical fitness is taught and practised. Schools can be certified at three levels.

## Basic Fit India School

A school needs:

- one PE-trained teacher who is physically fit and active
- a playground where two or more outdoor games are played
- one PE period daily for every section
- at least 60 minutes of daily physical activity for all students

The school certifies itself and registers online at fitindia.gov.in. A certificate is issued online. It may then use the Fit India logo and flag.

## Fit India 3 Star School

Everything in Basic, plus:

- all teachers spend 60 minutes or more each day on physical activity
- at least two teachers, including one PET, are trained in two sports each
- the school celebrates a Fit India School week
- facilities for four sports, including two outdoor sports
- every student learns and plays two sports; one can be a traditional or local game

## Fit India 5 Star School

Everything in 3 Star, plus:

- intra-school competitions every quarter, inter-school competitions and an Annual Sports Day
- all teachers trained in PE and two or more sports coaches
- a PE curriculum from NCERT, CBSE or SCERT
- a yearly fitness check of all children
- the playground opened to neighbours or parents (a small fee for upkeep is allowed)

## How a school applies for 3 or 5 Star

The school files a claim online. The Fit India Mission checks it and then gives an online certificate and a letter of praise. A printed copy is sent by post. For questions, the contact is contact[at]fitindia[dot]gov[dot]in.
