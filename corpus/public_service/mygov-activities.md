## What you can do on MyGov

MyGov is a citizen engagement platform of the Government of India. Its home page invites people to take part in good governance in several ways.

## MyGov tasks and contests

Under "Do", MyGov lists skill-building tasks, activities and contests, both online and on the ground. Several of these carry cash or other prizes.

## MyGov quizzes

MyGov runs government-backed quizzes on quiz.mygov.in. Some quizzes offer cash prizes or e-certificates. Check each quiz for who can take part and its last date.

## Discussions, polls, blogs and pledges on MyGov

On MyGov, citizens can share opinions on national issues in discussions, vote in polls and surveys, and write blogs. They can also take public pledges, such as a cyber safety pledge or an anti-tobacco pledge, and follow content such as Mann Ki Baat episodes.

## MyGov Prime

MyGov Prime is where citizens can contribute ideas through technology and innovation challenges.
