## What MyGov is

MyGov is the Government of India's Citizen Engagement Platform. It works with many government bodies and ministries to collect people's views on policies and on issues of public interest. It also shares information about government schemes and programmes.

## When MyGov started and who runs it

MyGov was launched on 26th July 2014. It is part of Digital India Corporation, a Section 8 company under the Ministry of Electronics and IT.

## How many people use MyGov

The MyGov overview page says it had 64.60 million registered users as on 1 September 2026.

## How citizens take part in MyGov

Citizens can take part through discussions, tasks, polls, surveys, blogs, talks, pledges, quizzes and on-ground activities. MyGov reaches people through the internet, mobile apps, IVRS, SMS and outbound dialling. Citizens have also designed logos through MyGov contests and given their views on draft policies.
