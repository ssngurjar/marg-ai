## What the LAMP Fellowship is

The LAMP Fellowship, run by PRS Legislative Research, places young graduates with a Member of Parliament (MP) to do research for them. It is a full-time role for the whole fellowship, not an internship. Fellows cannot take up other academic or professional work at the same time.

## LAMP Fellowship duration

The FAQ says: "The Fellowship starts from the middle of June with a rigorous 1-month long training." Fellows are then assigned to an MP during the Monsoon session of Parliament and continue until the end of the Budget session of the following year.

## LAMP Fellowship stipend

The FAQ says: "LAMP Fellows will receive a stipend of Rs. 23,000 per month during the course of the Fellowship." Fellows must be based in New Delhi for the whole fellowship and arrange their own accommodation. They use their own computer and internet. They get 2 days of leave per month.

## LAMP Fellowship eligibility

Candidates from any discipline with at least a Bachelor's degree can apply. For the 2026-27 fellowship, the FAQ says applicants "should be up to 25 years of age (date of birth on or after June 1, 2000)." Only Indian citizens are eligible; PIO and OCI cardholders cannot apply. Work experience is not required, and Master's degree holders get no advantage.

## LAMP Fellowship selection process

- The application form opens in December. Applicants submit two essays: a statement of purpose and a policy analysis.
- Shortlisted candidates take a compulsory online evaluation in January.
- Those who clear the online test are called for interviews.

The FAQ says applications for the LAMP Fellowship 2026-27 are closed.

## How MPs are assigned to LAMP Fellows

MPs are assigned randomly, across parties, both the Treasury and Opposition, and both Houses. A fellow's political views, education and ideology are not considered.

## After the LAMP Fellowship

The FAQ says former fellows have worked with MPs, ministers, political parties and consulting firms, gone on to higher studies in India and abroad, joined think tanks, multilateral bodies and companies, and some have joined the IAS or IFS.
