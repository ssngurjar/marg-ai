## LAMP Fellowship eligibility: age

The LAMP Fellowship page of PRS Legislative Research says: "LAMP Fellowship is open to candidates who are 25 years of age or below."

## LAMP Fellowship eligibility: education

The page says: "Candidate must have at least a Bachelor's degree in any academic discipline." So a school student cannot apply yet, but can plan for it after finishing a degree in any subject.

## LAMP Fellowship eligibility: citizenship

The page says: "Only Indian citizens are eligible for the LAMP Fellowship."

## What a school student can do now

The LAMP Fellowship needs a Bachelor's degree, so for a student in class 11 or 12 it is a goal for later. Any academic discipline is accepted, so the choice of college subject does not shut this door.
