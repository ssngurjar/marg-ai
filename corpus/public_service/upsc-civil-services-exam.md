## What the Civil Services Examination is

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) holds the Civil Services Examination (CSE) every year. These facts come from UPSC's Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE, dated 4 February 2026, for the Civil Services Examination, 2026. Each year has its own notice on upsc.gov.in, and the rules can change from year to year.

Through this one examination UPSC fills many services, including the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), the Indian Foreign Service (IFS), the Indian Police Service (IPS), the Indian Revenue Service, the Indian Postal Service and other Group 'A' and Group 'B' services. The 2026 notice expected approximately 933 vacancies.

## Who can apply: education

The notice says: "A candidate must hold a Graduate degree" from a university set up by an Act of the central or a state legislature, or a deemed university, "or possess an equivalent qualification". The notice names no particular subject for the degree.

Students who have not yet got their degree result, or who are going to appear in their final degree exam, may also apply for the written part. They must show proof of passing their degree if they qualify for the interview.

So the path is: finish class 12, then a graduation degree, then the Civil Services Examination.

## Who can apply: age

For the 2026 examination: "A candidate must have attained the age of 21 years and must not have attained the age of 32 years on the 1st of August, 2026". The upper age limit is relaxed:

- up to five years for Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates;
- up to three years for Other Backward Classes candidates eligible for reservation;
- up to 10 years for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities;
- and for some ex-servicemen and defence personnel, as the notice lists.

## Number of attempts

The notice says every eligible candidate "shall be permitted six (6) attempts at the CSE". The notice's table gives more attempts to some categories: unlimited for SC/ST candidates and 9 for OBC candidates; for Persons with Benchmark Disability, 9 for General, EWS and OBC candidates and unlimited for SC/ST. Sitting even one paper of the Preliminary Examination counts as an attempt.

## The stages of the examination

The examination has two stages:

1. The Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination: objective-type papers, to select candidates for the Main Examination. It has "two compulsory Papers of 200 marks each". General Studies Paper-II is a qualifying paper, with minimum qualifying marks fixed at 33%.
2. The Civil Services (Main) Examination: a written examination and an interview (Personality Test), to select candidates for the services.

## Fee

For the 2026 Preliminary Examination the fee was Rs. 100, and candidates admitted to the Main Examination paid a further Rs. 200. Female, SC, ST and Persons with Benchmark Disability candidates are exempted from the fee.

## How to apply

Candidates apply online at https://upsconline.nic.in. For 2026 the Preliminary Examination was held on 24 May 2026, so the next chance is the Civil Services Examination of a later year: its notice is published on upsc.gov.in.
