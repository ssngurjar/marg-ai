## What it is

ISRO, India's space agency, has a "Students" page that lists its programmes for students. Some are for school students and some are for college students.

## For school students

**ISRO STEM Portal.** An online portal with certified courses, video sessions with space scientists, a virtual museum, a space quiz, knowledge videos, "ISRO missions at Glance" and a space dictionary. Students can also submit their own ideas, such as apps or research papers.

**YUVIKA (Young Scientist Programme).** A programme for "School Children", with a preference for rural areas. It teaches basic knowledge of space technology, space science and their uses. Its goal is to "Catch them young" and encourage careers in science, technology, engineering and maths (STEM).

**School Bhuvan – NCERT.** A map-based learning portal about the country's natural resources, environment and sustainable development.

## For college students and institutions

**Student Satellite Programme.** Lets university and college students build student satellites and payloads and take part in ISRO missions, with ISRO coordinating.

**IIRS Dehradun e-learning.** Distance learning through live online classes and e-learning courses.

**CSSTEAP.** Postgraduate courses in remote sensing and GIS, satellite communications, satellite meteorology and global climate, and space and atmospheric sciences.

**Facility visits.** Colleges can arrange technical visits to ISRO facilities on selected days. The college must write to the centre's focal point for an appointment first.

## How to apply

The page does not give application steps or eligibility for these programmes. Check the ISRO website, www.isro.gov.in, for current announcements.
