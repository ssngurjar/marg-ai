## Nursing courses in India

The Indian Nursing Council (INC) sets who can join each nursing course. Its eligibility page lists, among others: ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife), GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery), B.Sc. (Nursing), Post Basic B.Sc. (Nursing) and M.Sc. (Nursing). Nursing is one way to work in health care besides becoming a doctor.

## ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife)

- Passed 10+2 in Arts or Science, with English Core or English Elective, or Health Care Science (Vocational), from a recognised board.
- 10+2 Arts or Science from the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) is accepted.
- "The minimum age for admission shall be 17 years on or before 31st December of the year in which admission is sought." The maximum age is 35.
- The candidate must be medically fit. Admission is once a year.

## GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery)

- "10+2 with English and must have obtained a minimum of 40% at the qualifying examination and English individually", from a recognised board. Science is preferred.
- Also eligible: 10+2 with English and 40% in Vocational ANM from a school recognised by the INC, or 40% in Vocational Health Care Science; and registered ANMs with a pass mark.
- NIOS and State Open School are accepted.
- Minimum age 17 years on 31 December of the year of admission; the upper limit is 35, with no age bar for ANM/LHV.
- There is a 5% relaxation for SC/ST candidates, and a 3% reservation for candidates with 40–50% locomotor disability of the lower extremity.

## B.Sc. (Nursing)

- "10+2 class passed with Science (PCB) & English Core/English Elective with aggregate of 45% marks" from a recognised board. PCB means Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
- NIOS: "Students appearing in 10+2 examination in Science conducted by National Institute of Open School with 45% marks" are eligible.
- "The minimum age for admission shall be 17 years on 31st December of the year in which admission is sought."
- The candidate must be medically fit. Admission is once a year.

## After B.Sc. Nursing

M.Sc. (Nursing) needs a B.Sc. Nursing (or Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing) with at least 55% aggregate, registration as a nurse and midwife with a State Nursing Registration Council, and at least one year of work experience.
