## What it is

The Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST) is listed under "Autonomous bodies" on the ISRO website. The page calls it "Asia's first Space University".

## What it teaches

IIST offers "undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral and post-doctoral programmes" in space science, space technology and space applications. So it teaches from a first degree all the way up to research.

## Where it is

- It was set up in 2007.
- Address: Valiamala P O, Thiruvananthapuram - 695547.
- The campus is about 20 km from Thiruvananthapuram city.
- It is a residential campus, so students live there.

## Teachers and departments

IIST has nearly 100 faculty members across seven departments.

## What the page does not say

This page does not say which entrance exam is used for admission, what the eligibility rules are, or whether graduates get jobs at ISRO. A student interested in IIST should check the institute's own admission notices for the current year before planning.
