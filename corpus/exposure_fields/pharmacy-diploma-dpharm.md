## Becoming a pharmacist

A pharmacist works with medicines, for example in a hospital or a medicine shop. These facts come from the Pharmacy Council of India's "Education Regulations, 2020 for Diploma course in Pharmacy", published in the Gazette of India.

The regulations say the minimum qualification to register as a pharmacist is "a pass in Diploma in Pharmacy (Part-I & Part-II) and satisfactory completion of Diploma in Pharmacy (Part-III)", or any other qualification the Pharmacy Council of India approves as equivalent.

## Who can join the Diploma in Pharmacy

"Minimum qualification for admission to Diploma in Pharmacy - A pass in 10+2 examination (science academic stream) with Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Mathematics", or any other qualification the Pharmacy Council of India approves as equivalent. So a student with either Biology or Mathematics in class 11-12 science can join. Seats are reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes as the governments direct.

## How long it takes

- "The duration of the course shall be for two academic years", each of at least 180 working days. These are Part-I and Part-II.
- After that, "five hundred hours of practical training spread over a period of not less than three months". This is Part-III, done under a registered pharmacist.
