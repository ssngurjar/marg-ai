## What it is

The National Institute of Design (NID) is a statutory institute under the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. The National Institute of Design Act 2014 recognises it as an Institution of National Importance. It is also recognised as a Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research.

## What kind of design it teaches

The page describes NID as a leading institution for:

- industrial design (designing products and objects)
- communication design (designing messages, visuals and media)
- textile design
- IT-integrated (experiential) design

The website lists programmes and international and collaborative programmes.

## Research centres

NID runs research centres including:

- the International Centre for Indian Crafts
- the Center for Bamboo Initiatives
- the Railway Design Center
- the Smart Handloom Innovation Centre
