## What it is

The National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) offers degrees in fashion design and fashion technology. This page lists its undergraduate (first degree) programmes.

## How long the programmes are

The page states that the B.Des. (Bachelor of Design) and B.F.Tech. (Bachelor of Fashion Technology) programmes are four years long. A Foundation Programme is also listed.

## B.Des. specialisations

NIFT lists seven design specialisations:

- Accessory Design
- Fashion Communication
- Fashion Design
- Fashion Interiors
- Knitwear Design
- Leather Design
- Textile Design

## B.F.Tech. specialisation

- Apparel Production

## What the page does not say

This page does not give the eligibility rules (such as class 12 subjects or age), the entrance test, or the admission dates. These change each year, so a student should check the Admissions section of www.nift.ac.in for the current year's notice before applying.
