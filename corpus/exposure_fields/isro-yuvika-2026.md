## What it is

YUVIKA (YUva VIgyani KAryakram, the Young Scientist Programme) is "a flagship outreach initiative of ISRO". It aims to build scientific curiosity in school students and give them an early look at space science, space technology and their uses.

## Who could take part in 2026

The 2026 programme was for "ninth class passed out school students of India", which means students who had passed class 9. The page does not give an age limit or explain how students were chosen. It says 456 "meritorious students" were selected from 1,06,530 registrations. They came from 28 states and 8 union territories.

## What happened

YUVIKA is a "two-week residential programme". Students stay at an ISRO centre. The 2026 programme ran from May 11 to 22, 2026. It was the sixth edition. Over the previous five editions, 1,320 students were trained.

## The nine host centres in 2026

- VSSC, Thiruvananthapuram
- SAC, Ahmedabad
- URSC, Bengaluru
- SDSC-SHAR, Sriharikota
- NRSC, Hyderabad
- IIRS, Dehradun
- NE-SAC, Shillong
- IPRC, Mahendragiri
- RRSC West, Jodhpur

## Is it open now?

No. The 2026 programme is over; it finished in May 2026. This page does not give dates for a next edition, how to register, or who pays for travel and stay. Students interested in a future YUVIKA should watch the ISRO website, www.isro.gov.in, for a new announcement.
