## What it is

The Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) has an Agricultural Education Division. Its job is to plan, promote and coordinate agricultural education in the country. It works to improve the quality and usefulness of higher agricultural education and to strengthen the system of agricultural universities.

## Where you can study agriculture

The page lists the universities that make up the agricultural university system:

- State Agricultural Universities: 62
- Deemed to be Universities: 5
- Central Agricultural Universities: 2
- Central Universities with Agriculture Faculty: 4

These make up the agricultural university system that ICAR works with.

## What the page does not say

The page does not name the entrance exams for these universities, the courses offered, or any scholarship amounts. Admission rules differ by university and year, so check the university's own admission notice before applying.
