## What it is
The National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) is a central government scholarship from the Ministry of Education, Department of School Education and Literacy. It is for bright students from poorer families, so that they do not drop out after class 8 and keep studying. These revised guidelines are dated March 2022 and came into effect from FY 2022-23. They say the scheme was approved for 2021-22 to 2025-26.

## Who can apply
- Parental income must not be more than Rs. 3,50,000 per annum from all sources.
- The student must study as a regular student in a Government, Government-aided or local body school.
- Students of Kendriya Vidyalayas, Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas, residential schools run by the Centre or State where boarding and lodging are given, and private schools are not eligible.
- To sit for the selection test in class 8, the student needs at least 55% marks (or equivalent grade) in class 7. SC/ST students get 5% relaxation.

## The selection exam
Each State/UT holds its own test in class 8. The guidelines say the test is held for class 8 students in November every year. There are two papers:
- Mental Ability Test (MAT): 90 multiple-choice questions on reasoning, such as analogy, classification, number series and patterns.
- Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT): 90 multiple-choice questions on science, social studies and mathematics from classes 7 and 8.
Each paper is 90 minutes. Children with disability get extra time as applicable. To be selected, a student must get at least 40% marks in the two tests taken together (32% for SC/ST students) and at least 55% marks in the class 8 exam (5% relaxation for SC/ST).

## How much it gives
The scholarship is Rs. 12000 per annum (Rs. 1000 per month). It starts in class 9 and can continue up to class 12, for a maximum of four years. A total of 1,00,000 fresh scholarships are given each year, divided among states. Rajasthan's quota in the guidelines is 5471.

## Keeping the scholarship
- Pass class 8 and get clear promotion to class 9.
- Get clear promotion from class 9 to 10, and from class 11 to 12, in the first attempt.
- Get at least 60% marks in class 10 (55% for SC/ST) to continue in classes 11 and 12.
- A gap of one academic year stops the scholarship, and it cannot be restarted.
- A student can take only one central government scholarship.

## How to apply
Selected students apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). The State verifies the application. The money is sent directly to the student's bank account by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Students must also renew on NSP every year within the given time, or the scholarship stops.
