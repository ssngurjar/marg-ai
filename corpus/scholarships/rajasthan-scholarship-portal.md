## What it is
The Social Justice and Empowerment Department of the Government of Rajasthan runs a scholarship portal for students who study after class 10. The application is online and paperless.

## Schemes on the portal for 2026-27
- Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes (SC)
- Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Tribes (ST)
- Post-Matric Scholarship for Most Backward Classes (MBC)
- Post-Matric Scholarship for Other Backward Classes (OBC)
- Dr. Ambedkar Post-Matric Scholarship for Economically Backward Class (EBC) students
- Dr. Ambedkar Post-Matric Scholarship for Denotified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes (DNT)
- Mukhyamantri Sarvjan Uchcha Shiksha Chhatravriti Yojna, for students at IITs, IIMs and other national-level institutions

## Who can apply
- You must be an original resident (mool nivasi) of Rajasthan.
- You must be studying at a state, autonomous or recognised private institution, or a national-level institution, inside or outside Rajasthan.
The portal page does not list income limits or amounts. These are in the scheme documents linked on the portal.

## How to apply
- Log in as a student through the Rajasthan SSO portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in).
- The portal has a page to check or register your Jan Aadhaar details.
- You can track your application on the "Scholarship Application Status" page.

## Dates
The portal says applications for the 2024-25 and 2025-26 sessions must be submitted by 30 November 2026. An opening order for 2026-27 is posted, but the page does not give its dates.
