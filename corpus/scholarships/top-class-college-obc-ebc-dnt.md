## What it is
The Central Sector Scheme of Top Class Education in College for OBC, EBC and DNT Students gives full financial support to students of these categories who get admission in a top institution after class 12. It is funded fully by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. These guidelines are "As amended from 2023-2024".

## Who can apply
- OBC, EBC or DNT students with total annual family income from all sources "up to Rs. 2.50 lakh".
- The student must have taken admission in a full-time course in an institution notified under the scheme, through that institution's normal selection process.
- Institutions that can be included are IIMs, IITs, IIITs, AIIMS, NITs, NIFTs, NIDs, Institutes of Hotel Management, National Law Universities and other central government institutions, and others on the NAAC A++ and A+ list, the top 100 NIRF list, or Institutions of National Importance.
- Each institution has a fixed number of places (slots). If more students are eligible, the top students on the admission merit list get them.
- 30% of each institution's slots are kept for girls.
- Not more than 2 siblings in a family can get it.
- Preference goes to OBC students, as most of the fund is for them.

## How much it gives
- Full tuition fee and non-refundable charges. For private institutions there is a ceiling of Rs. 2.00 lakh per annum per student.
- Living expenses at Rs. 3000 per month.
- Books and stationery at Rs. 5000 per annum.
- A computer or laptop with accessories, up to Rs. 45000 per student, once during the course.
The money is paid directly to the student by DBT. The student must pay the fee to the institute within a month of getting it.

## How to apply
Apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). Upload your income certificate and caste certificate, with your bank account details, admission rank and fee details. The institution checks and verifies the application on NSP. You must apply every year, also for renewal. Students who do not get a slot can still apply for the post-matric scholarship through their state, if eligible.

## Keeping it
The scholarship continues till the course ends if your performance is good. It stops if you fail to get promoted to the next semester or class.
