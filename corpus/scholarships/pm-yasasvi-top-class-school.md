## What it is
The Central Sector Scheme of Top Class Education in Schools for OBC, EBC and DNT students is run by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. It pays for a good school education for bright students from these categories, from class 9 until they finish class 12.

## Who can apply
- OBC, EBC and DNT students.
- Household annual income must be "less than Rs. 2.5 lakhs".
- The student must be studying in a "Top Class School" chosen under the scheme. These are schools that have always had 100% pass results in classes 10 and 12. They can be government, aided or private schools.
- Students already studying in these schools apply with the marks they got in the final exam of the previous class.

## How students are chosen
- The number of places for each state and each class is announced in April every year.
- At least 30% of the scholarships are kept for girls.
- A separate state-wise merit list is made for boys and girls in each class, based on the marks of the previous class. Scholarships are given by merit automatically.

## How much it gives
The money covers tuition fee, hostel fee and other charges the school asks for, up to a maximum of:
- Rs. 75,000 per annum per student in class 9 and 10
- Rs. 1,25,000 per annum per student in class 11 and 12

The money goes directly into the beneficiary's account (DBT), in one instalment, to be released before 15th August each year.

## How to apply
1. Open a bank account. You must give its details when you apply.
2. Apply on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) with your previous class marks.
3. The school's nodal officer verifies the application online, and the State Government confirms it.

A student who gets this scholarship cannot get help from another government scheme for the same costs.
