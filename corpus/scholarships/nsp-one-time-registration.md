## What it is
The National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at scholarships.gov.in is the Government of India website where students apply for many central and state scholarships. It is run by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. Students, schools, colleges and officers all use it.

## One Time Registration (OTR)
- To apply for a scholarship on NSP you need an OTR. The portal says OTR has been required since academic year 2024-25.
- OTR is a unique 14-digit number linked to your Aadhaar or Aadhaar Enrolment ID (EID).
- It stays valid for your whole time as a student, so you do not register again every year.
- You can make your OTR on the NSP website or with the "NSP OTR" app on the Google Play Store.

## How to apply
1. Generate your OTR.
2. Check which schemes you are eligible for.
3. Apply. For academic year 2026-27, the portal says you may apply for one merit-based scheme and one or more welfare-based schemes.
4. Track your application and your payment on the portal.

## Help near you
Common Service Centres (CSCs) can do the work for you, including making the OTR and filling the form, for a fixed fee of Rs 30 per candidate. You can find a nearby centre at locator.csccloud.in.

## Tips from the portal
- Students with disabilities must first log in to the UDID portal and agree to share their details before applying on NSP.
- Face authentication can fail if biometrics are locked in Aadhaar. Make sure the biometric lock status is "Unlocked" before trying.
- Error 904 during face authentication means you should update the UIDAI AadhaarFaceRD app.

## Dates
The portal opened for academic year 2026-27 on 1 June 2026. Each scheme has its own last date, shown on the portal. Always check the date for your scheme on the portal, because dates change every year.

## Getting help
The portal has an NSP Helpdesk, a PFMS Helpdesk for payment questions, a grievance registration page, and lists of nodal officers by scheme and by district.
