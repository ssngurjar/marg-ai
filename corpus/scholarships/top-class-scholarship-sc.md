## What it is
The Scheme of Top Class Scholarship for SC Students gives financial support to SC students who get admission in top institutions after class 12. It is funded by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. These guidelines are "Applicable from 2025-26".

## Who can apply
- SC students with total annual family income from all sources "up to Rs. 8.00 lakh".
- The student must have taken admission in a full-time course in an institution notified under the scheme.
- Fresh scholarships are given only to 1st year students.
- Each institution has a fixed number of places (slots). If more students are eligible, the top students on the admission merit list get them. If two students tie for the last slot, the one with lower family income gets it.
- 30% of each institution's slots are kept for SC girl students.
- Not more than 2 siblings in a family can get it.
- Institutions that can be included are IIMs, IITs, IIITs, AIIMS, NITs, NIFTs, NIDs, IHMs, NLUs and other central government institutions, and others on the NAAC A++ and A+ list, the top 100 NIRF list, or Institutions of National Importance.

## How much it gives
- Full tuition fee and non-refundable charges, with a ceiling of Rs. 2.00 lakh per annum per student for private institutions.
- An academic allowance of Rs. 86,000 in the first year and Rs. 41,000 in every year after that, for living costs, books, and a computer or laptop.
The money is paid directly to the student by DBT. The student must pay the fee to the institute within a month.

## How to apply
Apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). Upload your income certificate and caste certificate, with your bank account details, admission rank and fee details. The institution verifies the application. SC students who do not get a slot can still get the Post-Matric Scholarship through their state, if eligible.

## Keeping it
Once given, it continues till the course ends if performance is good. It stops if you are not promoted to the next semester or class, and starts again once you are promoted. If you change your institute after selection, the scholarship stops.
