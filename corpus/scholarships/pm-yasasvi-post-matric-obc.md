## What it is
The post-matric part of PM YASASVI gives money to OBC, EBC and DNT students studying after class 10, so they can finish their education. It is run by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment through the State Governments. These guidelines cover 2021-22 to 2025-26.

## Who can apply
- Indian nationals who belong to OBC, EBC or DNT as notified for their state, and who have passed class 10 (matriculation) or a higher exam.
- Parents' or guardian's income from all sources must not be more than "Rs. 2.50 lakh per annum". For working students, their own income plus parents' income must be within this limit.
- The course must be a recognised post-matric course in a recognised institution.
- Only two boys of the same parents can get it. There is no such limit for girls.
- Studying the same level again in another subject (for example B.Com after B.A.) is not allowed.
- The guidelines say class 11 of a "Higher Secondary School" course that runs straight on from class 10 is not covered, but students who join another course after passing class 10 are treated as post-matric. Ask your school which case applies to you.

## How much it gives
The amounts per year are given by course group:
- Group 1, degree and PG professional courses (medicine, engineering, management, law and others): academic allowance Rs 10000, tuition fee Rs 10000, total Rs 20000.
- Group 2, other professional courses (nursing, pharmacy, hotel management and others): Rs 8000 plus Rs 5000, total Rs 13000.
- Group 3, other graduate and PG courses (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com and others): Rs 6000 plus Rs 2000, total Rs 8000.
- Group 4, non-degree courses after class 10, such as senior secondary (class 11 and 12), ITI and 3-year polytechnic diplomas: Rs 5000 plus 0, total Rs 5000.
States may add more from their own money. 30% of funds are kept for girls and 5% for students with disabilities.

## How to apply
- Before admission, register for a Freeship Card on your state's scholarship portal. It lets you take admission without paying tuition and hostel fees first. You pay the college within 7 working days of getting the scholarship money.
- Documents may include a photo, mark sheets, caste certificate and income certificate. The income certificate is needed only once.
- The money is paid only through DBT into your account.
- The scholarship continues till the course ends, with good results and at least 75% attendance every year.
