## What it is
The Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (PM-USP CSSS) is given by the Ministry of Education, Department of Higher Education. It helps bright students from poor families meet part of their daily costs in college. It is based on class 12 board exam results. Up to 82,000 fresh scholarships are given each year. The guidelines apply from academic year 2022-23 onwards.

## Who can apply
- Students above the 80th percentile of successful candidates in their stream (Humanities, Science or Commerce) in their class 12 board exam.
- Gross parental or family income "upto ₹ 4.5 lakh per annum". The income certificate is needed only for fresh applicants.
- Studying a regular degree course, not correspondence or distance mode, and not a diploma.
- The college must be recognised by AICTE or the relevant regulatory body.
- Not taking any other merit scholarship, state scholarship, fee waiver or reimbursement scheme.
- 50% of scholarships are for girls. Reservation follows central rules: 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 27% OBC, and 5% for students with benchmark disability.

## How much it gives
- "₹12,000/-per annum" at graduation level for the first three years.
- "₹20,000/-per annum" at post-graduation level.
- For 5-year integrated or professional courses, ₹20,000 per annum in the 4th and 5th year.
- For B.Tech or B.Engg, ₹12,000 a year for the 1st to 3rd year and ₹20,000 in the 4th year.
The money goes directly into the student's own savings bank account by DBT. You can check payment status with "Know Your Payment" on the PFMS portal using Aadhaar number, bank account number or NSP application ID.

## How to apply
Apply online only, on the National Scholarship Portal (www.scholarships.gov.in), for fresh and renewal scholarships. Applications sent directly to the Ministry are not accepted. Your college verifies the application first, then the state.

## Keeping it
Renew every year on NSP before the cut-off date. You need at least 50% marks in the annual exam and at least 75% attendance. Ragging or criminal behaviour ends the scholarship.
