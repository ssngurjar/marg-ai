## What it is
The Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Scheduled Castes and Others helps families keep children in school and stop them dropping out. It is run by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment through the State Governments. These guidelines are dated March 2022 and cover 2021-22 to 2025-26. It has two parts.

## Who can apply
Part 1, for SC students:
- Studying in class 9 or 10, full time.
- Belong to a Scheduled Caste.
- Parent's or guardian's income must not be more than "Rs. 2.50 lakh per annum".

Part 2, for children of families in unclean and hazardous work, of any caste or religion:
- Studying in classes 1 to 10, full time.
- A parent or guardian is a manual scavenger, a tanner or flayer, a waste picker, or does hazardous cleaning.
- There is no family income limit.
- A certificate from the District Social Welfare Officer, or another officer named by the state, is needed.

The school must be a government school or a recognised private school with a valid UDISE code. The scholarship is for studies in India and is given by the state where the student lives.

## How much it gives
From 2022-23, a consolidated academic allowance:
- Day scholar: Rs. 3500 per annum
- Hosteller, Part 1: Rs. 7000 per annum
- Hosteller, Part 2: Rs. 8000 per annum (for class 3 to 10)
Students with disabilities get 10% extra.

## How to apply
- The school should make sure each student applies on the scholarship portal on the day of admission, and verifies the admission on the portal the same day.
- Renewal is automatic, based on attendance and the school confirming the student was promoted and rejoined.
- The money is paid only by DBT into the student's or parent's bank account, preferably Aadhaar-linked.
- A student can hold only one educational scholarship for classes 1 to 10, and must choose one if offered two.
