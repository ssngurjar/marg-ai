## What it is
The Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Rajasthan publishes this list of documents needed to apply for a scholarship on its department website. Most documents are taken automatically from Jan Aadhaar.

## Documents needed
1. Aadhaar card: taken from Jan Aadhaar
2. Jan Aadhaar card: entered by the student
3. Mool Niwas (domicile) certificate: taken from Jan Aadhaar
4. Income certificate: taken from Jan Aadhaar
5. Caste certificate: taken from Jan Aadhaar
6. Class 10 mark sheet: taken from DigiLocker or e-Vault
7. Mark sheet of the last exam passed: uploaded by the student
8. Fee receipt: uploaded by the student
9. Bank account details: taken from Jan Aadhaar

## Before you apply
- Update all your documents in Jan Aadhaar, e-Vault and DigiLocker before applying, so that there is no delay or trouble.
- Check your bank account details fully before applying, so that payment does not fail because the account is inactive or has a mistake.
