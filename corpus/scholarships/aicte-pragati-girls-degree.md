## What it is
The AICTE Pragati Scholarship Scheme for Girl Students (Degree) helps girls who study technical degree courses, such as engineering, in AICTE-approved institutions. It is run by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). The guideline document linked on the National Scholarship Portal is dated July 2020 and is marked "2020-21", so check the current notice on the AICTE website and NSP for any changes.

## Who can apply
- A girl admitted to the first year of a degree course, or to the second year through lateral entry, in an AICTE-approved institution.
- At most two girls per family.
- Family income from all sources "not be more than Rs. 8 lakh per annum" in the current financial year, with a valid income certificate from the State/UT government.
- The gap between passing the qualifying exam and admission should not be more than two years.

## How many and how chosen
- 5,000 scholarships a year, divided among states. Rajasthan's share in these guidelines is 152.
- All eligible girls from the north-eastern states and some UTs (including Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh) who apply get it, in addition to the 5,000.
- Selection is by merit, based on the percentage of marks in class 12 (10+2) or equivalent. Ties are broken by class 10 marks, then by age.
- Reservation is as per Government of India norms.

## How much it gives
"Rs. 50,000/- per annum" for every year of study: up to 4 years for first-year entrants and up to 3 years for lateral entry. It is a lump sum for college fee, computer, books, equipment and similar costs. No proof of spending is needed. It is paid by DBT once a year.

## How to apply
1. Apply online on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) when applications open. AICTE publishes a notice on its website.
2. Your institution verifies the application.
3. The state's Department of Technical Education checks it again.

## Keeping it
Renew every year on NSP with your pass certificate or mark sheet and a letter from the head of the institution. If you are not promoted to the next year, you lose the scholarship. You cannot take any other scholarship or stipend at the same time.
