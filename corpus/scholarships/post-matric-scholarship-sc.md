## What it is
The Post Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste students (PMS-SC) helps SC students study after class 10, in class 11 and 12, diplomas, ITI, college and higher. It is run by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment through the State Governments. These guidelines are dated March 2021 and cover 2020-21 to 2025-26.

## Who can apply
- Indian students who belong to a Scheduled Caste of the state where they live (domicile), and who have passed class 10 or a higher exam.
- Parents' or guardian's income from all sources must not be more than "Rs. 2,50,000/- (Rupees Two lakh Fifty thousand only)" in the last financial year.
- All the children of a family can get it.
- The course must be in a recognised institution. Recognised schools and colleges for class 11 and 12 are covered.
- Studying the same level again in another subject (for example B.Com after B.A.) is not allowed.
- Seats taken through management quota, NRI quota or spot admission are not covered.

## How much it gives
The scholarship pays compulsory non-refundable fees, including tuition fee as fixed by the state's fee committee, plus a yearly academic allowance:
- Group 1, degree and PG professional courses: Rs 13500 for hostellers, Rs 7000 for day scholars.
- Group 2, other professional courses: Rs 9500 hostellers, Rs 6500 day scholars.
- Group 3, other graduate and PG courses (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com and others): Rs 6000 hostellers, Rs 3000 day scholars.
- Group 4, non-degree courses after class 10, such as class 11 and 12, ITI and 3-year polytechnic diplomas: Rs 4000 hostellers, Rs 2500 day scholars.
Divyang (disabled) students get 10% extra. The guidelines say these allowances apply from 2021-22 and are linked to the price index, with yearly revisions, so the current amount can be higher. States may also add more.

## How to apply
- Before admission, register for a Freeship Card on your state's scholarship portal. It lets you take admission without paying tuition and hostel fees first. You must pay the institution within 7 working days of getting the scholarship money.
- Documents may include a passport-size photo, certificates of exams passed, caste certificate and income certificate. The income certificate is needed only once, at admission.
- Apply at the time of admission. The money is paid by DBT into your account.
