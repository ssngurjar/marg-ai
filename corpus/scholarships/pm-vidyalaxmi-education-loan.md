## What it is
PM-Vidyalaxmi is a Central Sector Scheme approved by the Union Cabinet on 6 November 2024. It helps bright students who are short of money to get education loans for higher studies. The whole process is digital. It is expected to cover more than 22 lakh students each year.

## Who can get it
Students who get admission in top quality Higher Educational Institutions (QHEIs) of the country. Loans are given by Scheduled Banks, Regional Rural Banks and Cooperative Banks.

## What it gives
- Loans are collateral-free and guarantor-free. You do not have to pledge land or property, and you do not need someone to stand guarantee.
- For loans up to ₹7.5 lakh, the Government of India gives a 75% credit guarantee to the bank.
- If your family income is up to ₹8 lakh a year, you get a 3% interest subvention (the government pays part of the interest) on loans up to ₹10 lakh.
- If your family income is up to ₹4.5 lakh a year, you get full interest subvention, as the page says is already in place.

## How to apply
Apply on the PM-Vidyalaxmi portal (pmvidyalaxmi.co.in). The process is fully digital. For interest subvention for FY 2025-26, claims can be made through the bank or branch that gave the loan between 01.09.2026 and 31.12.2026. If you used the PM-Vidyalaxmi portal, you must choose interest subvention there.
