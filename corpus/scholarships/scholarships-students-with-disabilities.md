## What it is
The Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD), Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, runs an umbrella scheme "Scholarships for Students with Disabilities". These revised guidelines are dated 25 September 2024. The scheme has six parts. For school students the main ones are:
- Pre-matric scholarship, for classes 9 and 10
- Post-matric scholarship, for class 11 up to post-graduate degree or diploma
- Top Class Education, for degree and PG courses in institutes notified by DEPwD

## Who can apply
- Indian nationals with benchmark disability, that is 40% or more disability as defined in the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
- A valid disability certificate, Aadhaar-based authentication, and a UDID (Unique Disability ID).
- Parental income ceiling: "Rs. 2.5 lakh" per annum for pre-matric and post-matric, "Rs. 8 lakh" for Top Class.
- Pre-matric: a regular, full-time student in class 9 or 10 in a government school or a school recognised by the government or a Central/State board.
- Not more than two children with disabilities of the same parents can get it (a twin second child is allowed).
- A student cannot take any other scholarship or stipend at the same time.
- 50% of places are kept for girls. Places are limited: 25,000 for pre-matric and 17,000 for post-matric, including renewals.

## How much it gives
Pre-matric (class 9 and 10):
- Maintenance allowance: Rs.800 per month for hostellers, Rs. 500 per month for day-scholars
- Disability allowance: Rs. 4,000 per annum for visual impairment or intellectual disability; Rs 2000 per annum for all other disabilities
- Book allowance: Rs. 1,000 per annum

Post-matric (class 11 onwards):
- Admission and tuition fee: up to Rs. 1.40 lakh per annum (actual amount)
- Other compulsory fees: up to Rs 10,000 per annum (actual amount)
- Maintenance allowance: Rs.1,200 per month for hostellers, Rs. 650 per month for day-scholars
- Disability allowance: Rs. 4,000 or Rs 2000 per annum, as above
- Book allowance: Rs. 1,500 per annum

## How to apply
Apply online on the National Scholarship Portal (www.scholarships.gov.in) before the last date. Upload a photograph, proof of age, disability certificate, parent's income certificate, tuition fee receipt and last exam certificate. Your school or college verifies the application, and the state nodal officer processes it.

## Keeping it
The scholarship is renewed each year if you are promoted to the next class, with good conduct and regular attendance. If you have to repeat a class, you do not get it again for that class.
