## What it is
The Ministry of Labour and Employment gives Financial Assistance for Education (a scholarship) to the children of workers in beedi making, cinema, iron ore, manganese ore and chrome ore mines (IOMC), limestone and dolomite mines (LSDM), and mica work. It covers school (pre-matric) and studies after class 10 (post-matric). The amount ranges from "Rs. 1,000/- to Rs. 25,000/- per student per annum".

## Who can apply
- Either parent must be a beedi, cine, IOMC, LSDM or mica worker with at least six months of service. Contract and Gharkhata (home-based) workers are also eligible.
- Family income must be within "Rs. 21,000 per month or Rs. 2,52,000 per annum".
- The student must have passed the last exam in the first attempt (students promoted to the next class are also eligible).
- The student must have regular admission in a recognised Government or Government-aided institute in India. Correspondence courses are not covered.
- The student needs an active mobile number, Aadhaar linked to that mobile, and an Aadhaar-seeded bank account mapped with NPCI. A student without Aadhaar can use an Aadhaar Enrolment ID, or for a minor the parent's Aadhaar, as the guidelines explain.
- This is a welfare-based scheme, so a student can take it along with one merit-based scholarship.

## How much it gives (per year)
- Class 1 to 4 (for dress, books etc.): Rs 1000
- Class 5 to 8: Rs 1500
- Class 9: Rs 2000
- Class 10: Rs 2000
- Class 11 to 12: Rs 3000
- ITI: Rs 6000
- Polytechnic: Rs 6000
- Degree courses (UG and PG): Rs 6000
- Professional courses, such as engineering through JEE, MBBS through NEET, management or law: Rs 25000
Payment depends on the budget available.

## Documents needed
- The worker's valid identity card as a beedi, cine, IOMC, LSDM or mica worker.
- An income certificate from the competent authority in the district or tehsil.

## How to apply
Apply online only, on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in). NSP announces the opening and closing dates. The money is paid by DBT into the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account. If the bank account is not Aadhaar-seeded or not NPCI-mapped, payment can fail, so check it before applying.

## When it can be cancelled
If it was taken with false information, if studies stop, if the course or institution is changed without approval, or if the parent stops being such a worker.
