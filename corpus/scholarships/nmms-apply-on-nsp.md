## What it is
This is the official question-and-answer sheet from the Ministry of Education for students applying online for the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

## Who can apply
Students of any community who study in Government, Government-aided or local body schools can apply. Parental income from all sources must not be more than "Rs. 3,50,000/- per annum". To sit for the selection test in class 8, a student needs at least 55% marks (or equivalent grade) in class 7, relaxable for SC/ST students. Each State/UT holds its own selection test in class 8, with a Mental Ability Test (MAT) and a Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT), 90 minutes each.

## How much it gives
The scholarship amount is Rs. 12000 per annum. It is paid for classes 9 to 12, a maximum of four years, for studies in India only. It is not paid for diploma or certificate courses.

## How to apply
1. Register on the NSP at www.scholarships.gov.in to get an OTR (One-Time Registration) ID.
2. Log in to the NSP application form with the OTR. Your details and photo are taken from your OTR automatically.
3. Apply for a fresh or renewal NMMSS scholarship.

OTR is a unique number. You register with your mobile number and complete eKYC. The OTR number is sent to your phone and stays valid for your whole time as a student, so you do not register again.

## Documents needed
- Two documents are uploaded on the portal: Income Certificate and Caste Certificate.
- For school verification of a fresh application: the parent's or guardian's Income Certificate from the competent authority in the State/UT, and the Caste Certificate if applicable.

## Common problems
- To change your name or other personal details, first update them in Aadhaar. They then reach your OTR profile through a fresh eKYC.
- You cannot change your domicile state or scholarship category while any application is active. You must withdraw all applications first.
- You cannot withdraw an application after it is approved by the nodal officer (L2) or after payment is released.
- To change your password, log in to OTR.
