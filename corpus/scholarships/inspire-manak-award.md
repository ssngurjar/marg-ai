## What it is
INSPIRE-MANAK is an award for school students who come up with original ideas or innovations based on science that can help society. It is run by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, and carried out by the National Innovation Foundation – India (NIF). It is part of the INSPIRE scheme.

## Who can take part
- Students in classes 6 to 12.
- The student does not apply alone. The school nominates the ideas.
- Each school can send up to five ideas. Not more than two of them can be from classes 11 or 12, and those must be from the Science stream.
- Ideas can be written in any Indian language.

## How it works
1. The school holds its own idea competition and the principal sends the best five ideas online on the E-MIAS portal.
2. NIF shortlists the top 1,00,000 ideas.
3. Shortlisted students get the award money.
4. District-level exhibitions pick 10,000 ideas for the state level.
5. State-level exhibitions pick the top 1,000 ideas for the national level.
6. At the national exhibition, the top 60 ideas are considered for national awards, and NIF helps develop them into products.

Ideas are judged on newness, usefulness to society, being good for the environment, being easy to use, and being better than what already exists.

## How much it gives
Each shortlisted student gets INR 10,000, sent directly by DBT.
