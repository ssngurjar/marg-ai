## What it is
PM YASASVI (PM Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India for OBCs and Others) is an umbrella scheme of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. Its pre-matric part gives money to OBC, EBC and DNT students in classes 9 and 10. It is run through the State Governments. These guidelines cover 2021-22 to 2025-26.

## Who can apply
- The student must belong to OBC, EBC or DNT (DNT students not covered under SC, ST or OBC), with a valid certificate.
- Parents' or guardian's income from all sources must not be more than "Rs.2,50,000/- per annum".
- The student must study in class 9 or 10, full time, in a Government School only.
- The student must be an Indian national. The scholarship is for studies in India and is given by the state where the student lives (domicile).
- A student cannot hold another educational scholarship for class 9 and 10 at the same time. If offered two, the student picks one.

## How much it gives
A consolidated academic allowance of "Rs. 4000/- per annum".

## How to apply
- The state sets the detailed procedure. The guidelines say the school should make sure every admitted student applies on the scholarship portal on the same day as admission, and the school verifies it on the portal.
- An income certificate is needed only once, at the time of admission.
- The money is paid only through DBT into the student's account.

## Keeping it
The scholarship is renewed until the student passes class 10, if academic performance is good and attendance is at least 75% every year. Attendance is checked by an Aadhaar-based attendance system. If a student repeats a class, the scholarship for that class is given only once.
