## What it is

Under the Startup India programme, a business can be officially recognised as a startup by DPIIT (the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade). Recognition gives the business certain benefits. Recognition is for a registered business, so it is something a young person can use later, after starting a company or firm.

## Who can be recognised (Normal track)

- Age of the business: "The period of existence and operations should not exceed 10 years from the date of incorporation."
- Type: a Private Limited Company, a Registered Partnership Firm, an LLP, or a Cooperative Society.
- Turnover: "The annual turnover should not exceed Rs. 200 crore in any financial year since incorporation."
- It must be original: "The entity should not have been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business."
- It must be innovative or scalable: it should work on developing or improving a product, process or service, and/or have a business model that can grow and create jobs.

## Deeptech track

For deeptech startups the business can be up to 20 years old from incorporation, and turnover should not exceed Rs. 300 crore in any financial year. The other rules are the same.

## Benefits listed on the page

- Self-certification for 6 labour laws and 3 environmental laws, with labour-law inspections barred for 5 years (subject to conditions).
- Faster patent examination, government-paid facilitator fees, and an 80% rebate on patent filing.
- Income tax exemption under Section 80IAC for 3 consecutive financial years out of the first 10 years. Only Private Limited Companies and LLPs incorporated after 1st April, 2016 qualify.
- Winding up within 90 days of an insolvency application, under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.
- Easier public procurement: listing on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM), relaxed experience and turnover rules, and exemption from earnest money deposit (EMD).

## How to apply

1. Register or log in on the Startup India portal.
2. Click "Get Recognised".
3. For the Section 80IAC tax exemption, apply separately after recognition, uploading the Memorandum of Association or LLP Deed, any board resolution, and the last three years of accounts and income tax returns.
