## What it is

Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs) are labs set up in schools across India by the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM). The page says "Atal Innovation Mission is establishing Atal Tinkering Laboratories (ATLs) in schools across India."

## Why they exist

The scheme aims to "foster curiosity, creativity, and imagination in young minds". It builds skills such as:

- a design mindset (thinking about how to make things better for people)
- computational thinking (breaking a problem into steps a computer could follow)
- adaptive learning
- physical computing (making electronics and machines that respond to the world)

## How big it is

The page gives these numbers:

- 10,000 ATLs
- 35 states and union territories, covering 722 districts
- 6200+ Mentors of Change engaged
- 1.1 crore+ students actively engaged
- 60%+ of ATLs are in government or government-aided schools
- 96% of ATLs are in girls' or co-ed schools
- 16 lakh+ innovation projects created

## What a student can do

The page does not explain how a student joins an ATL or which classes use it. A student can find out whether their own school, or a nearby school, has an Atal Tinkering Lab, and ask the teacher in charge about using it. More about the labs is on aim.gov.in.
