## What it is

Startup India is the Government of India's portal for people who start or run startups. It shares resources for each stage of building a business, from the first idea onward. The page says its startup community is "600K strong".

## Getting recognised

The page says "eligible companies can get recognised as Startups by DPIIT" (the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade). Recognition gives access to tax benefits, simpler compliance and faster processing of intellectual property (patents and trademarks). The detailed rules are on the portal's "Benefits of Startup Recognition" page. For some resources, you may be asked to register on the Startup India portal.

## Startup India Seed Fund

The Startup India Seed Fund Scheme "aims to provide financial assistance to startups for proof of concept, prototype development" and later stages. The page says it supports proof of concept, building a prototype, product trials, market entry and commercialisation. Details are at seedfund.startupindia.gov.in.

## Learning and networking

- At the ideation (idea) stage, the portal lists "Online Courses" for people working on bringing an idea to life.
- Founders can also register on BHASKAR, a networking platform on the portal.

## Help

The portal's toll-free helpline is 1800 115 565, open 10:00 AM to 05:30 PM.
