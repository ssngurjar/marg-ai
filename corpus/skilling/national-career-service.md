## What it is

The National Career Service (NCS) is a portal of the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India. It calls itself "a one-stop solution offering a wide range of employment and career-related services."

## What you can do on it

- Search for jobs in India and abroad, and get matched to jobs
- Get career counselling and guidance from career counsellors ("Find Counsellor")
- Read career information and government schemes
- Find job fairs and register for them
- Find skill courses
- Use tools such as AI resume building, internships, an employability assessment, online mentoring, instructional videos and the NCS Skill Quotient test

The page lists job categories such as IT and communication, transport and storage, education, manufacturing, finance and insurance, health, power and energy, and hospitality. It also has filters for jobs for women and jobs for ex-servicemen. It links to schemes including the PM Internship Scheme and APAAR.

## Who can register

Jobseekers, employers, skill providers, career counsellors, local service providers, career centres, placement organisations, households and government departments.

## It is free

NCS "does not charge any fee at any stage of registration, job application, or interview processing." Jobseekers should not pay fees to employers. Check information only on the official website, www.ncs.gov.in.

## How to register

Choose your role (for example, Jobseeker), select "Get Started", then "Register". A registration flowchart can be downloaded from the same menu.
