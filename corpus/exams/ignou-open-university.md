## What IGNOU is

IGNOU is the Indira Gandhi National Open University. Its website says it was "established by an Act of Parliament in 1985", with the mission "to democratize education in India and beyond". It lets students study for a degree, diploma or certificate through open and distance learning, and through online programmes.

## IGNOU centres across India

The IGNOU website says it has over 69 Regional Centres, more than 2,000 Learner Support Centres across India, and 25 Overseas Study Centres in 15 countries. It has 21 Schools of Studies, from the School of Agriculture to the School of Vocational Education and Training.

## Programmes at IGNOU

The IGNOU website says it offers 325 programmes, including Four-Year Undergraduate Programmes (FYUP) and skill-based courses. It also offers 45 online academic programmes through its own learning system, and 268 courses on SWAYAM.

## How to apply to IGNOU

The IGNOU home page links to separate admission portals: one for open and distance learning (ODL) programmes and one for online programmes. Eligibility, fees and dates for each programme are given on those portals and in IGNOU's Common Prospectus, not on the home page.

## IGNOU rankings

The IGNOU website says NIRF ranks it first among open universities, and that NAAC gave it an A++ grade.
