## How many existing Sainik Schools there are

The "Existing Schools" page of the Sainik Schools Society lists 33 Sainik Schools. New Sainik Schools are listed on a separate page of the same website.

## Towns with an existing Sainik School

The page names each school by its town: Satara, Kunjpura, Balachadi, Kapurthala, Korukonda, Kazhakootam, Rewa, Bhubaneswar, Amaravathinagar, Purulia, Tilaiya, Bijapur, Chittorgarh, Goalpara, Ghorakhal, Nagrota, Imphal, Sujanpur Tira, Chandrapur, Punglwa, Gopalganj, Nalanda, Kodagu, Ambikapur, Sambalpur, Rewari, Jhunjhunu, Kalikiri, East Siang, Chhingchhip, Mainpuri, Jhansi and Amethi.
