## What the NIOS On-Demand Examination is

The NIOS On-Demand Examination (ODES) lets a NIOS student sit for an exam when they feel ready, instead of waiting for a fixed exam date. NIOS reintroduced it at Secondary level in 2005 and started it at Senior Secondary level in October 2007.

## Who can take the NIOS On-Demand Examination

Two kinds of learners can take it:

- Existing learners who have already taken admission in NIOS.
- New learners who have not taken admission in NIOS. They take a separate online admission for Secondary or for Senior Secondary.

## Subjects in the NIOS On-Demand Examination

Secondary level: Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science and Technology, Social Science, Economics, Business Studies, Home Science, Psychology, Indian Culture and Heritage, Painting, Data Entry Operations, Accountancy, Bharatiya Darshan and Sanskrit Sahitya.

Senior Secondary level: Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Political Science, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy, Home Science, Psychology, Sociology, Painting, Environmental Science, Mass Communication, Data Entry Operations and Introduction to Law.

## How the NIOS On-Demand Examination works

- A computer makes each question paper from a question bank. Papers differ but are of similar difficulty.
- Question papers are in English and Hindi. Candidates studying in a regional medium may answer in their regional language.
- The page says: "There will be no On-Demand Examination during the month of April,May,October and November."
- Theory exams are held at KVS centres and at NIOS headquarters in Noida, from 2:00 pm to 5:00 pm on set weekdays. Practical exams are held at a NIOS study centre or another chosen school.
- A student can reappear as many times as they want until they are happy with their marks.

## NIOS On-Demand Examination results

Results are published every month on the NIOS website, in the last week of the month, for exams held in the month before. Re-evaluation of answer scripts is allowed.
