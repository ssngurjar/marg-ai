## Kinds of programmes at IGNOU

The IGNOU admission portal's programme list includes these kinds of programmes:

- Bachelor's degrees, for example Bachelor of Arts
- Master's degrees, for example Master of Business Administration
- Diplomas, for example Diploma in Aquaculture
- Certificates, for example Certificate in French Language
- Advanced Certificates, for example Advance Certificate in Information Security
- PG Diplomas and PG Certificates
- Appreciation Courses and Awareness Programmes, for example Appreciation Course on Environment
- Micro Credential Courses

## IGNOU bachelor's programmes and their length

Examples of bachelor's programmes on the IGNOU admission portal, each lasting 3 years:

- BAM — Bachelor of Arts
- BAFEC — Bachelor of Arts (Economics)
- BAFEG — Bachelor of Arts (English)
- BCOMF — Bachelor of Commerce

## Eligibility and fees for IGNOU programmes

The programme list does not show eligibility or fees. Each programme has its own "Read More" page on the IGNOU admission portal with those details.
