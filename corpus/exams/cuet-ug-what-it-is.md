## What CUET-UG is

CUET-UG is the Common University Entrance Test for Undergraduate (UG) programmes. The National Testing Agency (NTA) has been given the task of conducting it. The NTA was set up by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, as an independent, autonomous testing organization.

## What CUET-UG scores are used for

CUET-UG scores are used for admission to undergraduate programmes in all Central Universities and in Participating Universities across the country. Participating universities can be State, Deemed or Private universities.

## Why CUET-UG helps rural students

The official CUET website says the test gives a common platform and equal opportunities to candidates across the country, especially those from rural and other remote areas, and helps them connect better with universities. A single examination lets a student reach many central and participating universities and be part of their admission process.

## Where to find eligibility and dates

The introduction page of the CUET website does not list eligibility or dates. These are given in the Information Bulletin published each year on cuet.nta.nic.in. Always check the bulletin for the current year before applying.
