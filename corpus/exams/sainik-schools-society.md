## Who runs Sainik Schools

Sainik Schools are managed by the Sainik Schools Society, under the Ministry of Defence. The Society is a registered society under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860, and it manages Sainik Schools in various parts of the country. The Society says the schools give quality education and training to cadets, preparing them to excel in all areas of life.

## Which classes Sainik Schools admit to

The Sainik Schools Society website says admission is offered at Class VI and Class IX. So a student in class 5 can prepare to join in class 6, and a student in class 8 can prepare to join in class 9.

## How students are selected for Sainik Schools

Admission is based on performance in the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE). The Society's website tells candidates to read the AISSEE Information Bulletin for eligibility, exam date, application fee, scheme of the exam, exam cities and how to apply. The bulletin is published every year, so always check the latest one.
