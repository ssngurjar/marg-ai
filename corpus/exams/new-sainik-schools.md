## What the new Sainik Schools scheme is

The Government of India approved the setting up of 100 new Sainik Schools under the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence. These schools are set up in partnership with States, NGOs and private partners, and are affiliated to the Sainik Schools Society.

## Which schools can become a new Sainik School

The Sainik Schools Society page says many kinds of schools can take part: government and government-aided schools, schools run by autonomous bodies such as KVS and NVS, schools run by government departments, schools managed by PSUs, schools run by NGOs, trusts or societies, and private schools. Priority is given to districts that do not already have a Sainik School.

## Fee support in new Sainik Schools

The Government of India gives "fee support for 50% of fees subject to an upper limit of Rs 40,000/- per annum". According to the page, this support is for 50% of the class strength, or a maximum of 50 students per year, on a merit-cum-means basis, and it covers Class 6 to Class 12.

## What this page does not say

The new schools page does not describe the admission test for new Sainik Schools. Ask the school, or check the Sainik Schools Society notifications, for how admission works there.
