## What the National Testing Agency (NTA) is

The National Testing Agency (NTA) describes itself as a premier, specialist, autonomous and self-sustained testing organization. Its job is to conduct entrance examinations for admission or fellowship in higher educational institutions. Many of India's big national entrance exams are run by the NTA, and each exam has its own website.

## Big NTA entrance exams and their websites

The NTA website lists these exams, among others:

- NEET — National Eligibility cum Entrance Test. Website: neet.nta.nic.in
- JEE — Joint Entrance Examination. Website: jeemain.nta.nic.in
- CUET-UG — Common University Entrance Test for undergraduate programmes. Website: cuet.nta.nic.in
- ICAR AIEEA — ICAR's All India Entrance Examination.
- NCET — National Common Entrance Test.
- NIFTEE — National Institute of Fashion Technology Entrance Examination.
- NCHM-JEE — Hotel Management Joint Entrance Examination.
- JIPMAT — Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test.

The NTA home page does not give the eligibility rules or dates. Each exam publishes these on its own website and in its information bulletin.

## NTA exams for admission to special schools

The NTA website also lists these entrance exams for schools:

- AISSEE — All India Sainik School Entrance Exam.
- RIMCEE — Rashtriya Indian Military College Entrance Examination.
- RMS CET — Rashtriya Military School Common Entrance Test.
- SHRESHTA-NETS — Residential Education for Students in High Schools in Targeted Areas.

Check each exam's own page for which class it admits to and who can apply.

## Other exams the NTA runs

The NTA list also includes UGC-NET, CUET-PG, Joint CSIR-UGC NET, CMAT, AIAPGET, GAT-B and BET, NTET (National Teachers Eligibility Test), NSSNET, and exams for SWAYAM courses.
