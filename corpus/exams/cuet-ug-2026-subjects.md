## CUET-UG 2026 subjects: three kinds

For the 2026 session, the official CUET-UG syllabus page lists 37 subjects in three groups:

- Languages: 13 subjects
- Domain subjects: 23 subjects
- Aptitude Test: 1 subject, the General Aptitude Test

Each subject has a subject code and its own syllabus file on the CUET website.

## CUET-UG 2026 language subjects

For the 2026 session, the 13 language subjects listed were English, Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu.

## CUET-UG 2026 domain subjects

For the 2026 session, the 23 domain subjects include Accountancy/Book-Keeping, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, History, Psychology and Sociology. The full list and each subject's syllabus are on the official CUET syllabus page.

## CUET-UG General Aptitude Test

The 2026 list has one aptitude subject, the General Aptitude Test. The syllabus page does not describe its contents; its syllabus file is linked on the CUET website.

## Note on the year

This list is for the CUET-UG 2026 session. Subjects can change from year to year, so check the syllabus page on cuet.nta.nic.in for the year you are applying.
