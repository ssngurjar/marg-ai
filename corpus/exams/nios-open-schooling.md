## What NIOS is

NIOS is the National Institute of Open Schooling. It began in 1979 as a project of CBSE and was renamed the National Institute of Open Schooling in 2002. NIOS gives school-stage education through open learning. Its profile page says NIOS is an "Open School" that serves "a heterogeneous group of learners up to pre-degree level", including children, neo-literates, and school drop-outs and left-outs. This makes it an option for a student who has left regular school or cannot attend one.

## Courses NIOS offers

The NIOS profile page lists these programmes, all taught through open and distance learning:

- Open Basic Education
- Secondary courses
- Senior Secondary courses
- Vocational Education
- Life Enrichment Programmes

## NIOS exams: more than one chance

The NIOS profile page says: "A learner is extended as many as nine chances to appear in public examinations spread over a period of five years." So a NIOS learner does not have to pass every subject in one go.

## How big NIOS is

NIOS works through five Departments, 23 Regional Centres, two Sub Regional Centres, two NIOS Cells and over 7,400 Study Centres. The profile page says "NIOS is the largest Open Schooling system in the world with cumulative enrolment of 4.13 million (during last 5 years)."
